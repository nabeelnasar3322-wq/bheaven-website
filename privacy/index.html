B·Heaven by Barceló

Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information we collect when you use bheavendubai.com, why, and what choices you have. B·Heaven is the rooftop restaurant and lounge at Barceló Al Jaddaf, Oud Metha Road, Al Jaddaf, Dubai, United Arab Emirates.

1. Table requests

When you use the reservation form on the homepage, the name, phone number, date, time, number of guests, seating and any note you add are put into a WhatsApp message that you send yourself. We use these details only to confirm your table. We do not store them on this website, and we do not share them with anyone outside our team.

2. Cookies and analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how the site is used, which pages are popular and where visitors come from. Google Analytics sets cookies, so it runs only if you tap Accept on the cookie banner. If you tap Decline, Google Analytics does not load and no analytics cookies are set. Your choice is saved in your browser's local storage on this device. You can change it at any time using the button below.

3. Anonymous site counts

Separately from Google Analytics, the menu, shisha, business lunch and homepage record anonymous counts, such as how often a dish is opened or a table request is started. These counts are not linked to a person and do not use cookies. A random session ID is kept in your browser's session storage only until you close the tab.

4. Who we share information with

We use Google (Analytics), Vercel (website hosting) and WhatsApp (Meta) to deliver the site and to talk to you. Google receives analytics data only when you accept analytics. Table requests reach us through WhatsApp, which is governed by its own privacy terms.

5. How long we keep information

Google Analytics retains data under its own settings. Anonymous site counts are kept as daily totals. Table request messages are kept in our WhatsApp chats for as long as needed to run the restaurant and to meet our legal record-keeping obligations.

6. Your rights

Under the UAE Personal Data Protection Law (Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021), you can ask us what personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, and object to certain uses. To make a request, email aljaddaf.bheaven@barcelo.com or call +971 50 612 9150.

7. Children

B·Heaven serves alcohol and is for guests aged 21 and over. Please do not send us personal information about anyone under 21 for a table request.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
