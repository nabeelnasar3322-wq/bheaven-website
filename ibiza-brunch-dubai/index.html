Sunday Unlimited Brunch in Dubai

The Ibiza Brunch at B-Heaven is Dubai's most spirited Sunday rooftop experience. Every Sunday from 1 PM, the rooftop terrace of Barceló Al Jaddaf becomes an open-air party with the full Dubai skyline as your backdrop.

Unlimited Levantine and Mediterranean plates come out of the charcoal kitchen, the live BBQ fires up poolside, and the DJ keeps the energy going all afternoon. Kids under 5 eat free; under-12s at half price — it's a full Sunday for the whole group.

Packages start from AED 149. Book ahead — Sundays fill fast.

Ibiza Brunch Dubai — FAQs

When is the Ibiza Brunch at B-Heaven?

Ibiza Brunch takes place every Sunday from 1 PM to 5 PM at B-Heaven rooftop, Barceló Al Jaddaf, Dubai.

What is included in the Ibiza Brunch?

The brunch includes unlimited food and drinks (packages vary), a live BBQ station, pool entry at Barceló Al Jaddaf, and DJ music throughout the afternoon.

How much does the Ibiza Brunch cost?

Brunch packages start from AED 149 per person. Kids under 5 are free; kids under 12 are half price. Contact us on WhatsApp for the full package menu and current pricing.

How do I book the Sunday brunch in Dubai?

Book instantly via WhatsApp at +971 50 612 9150. Advance booking is recommended as tables fill quickly every Sunday.

Is there a pool at the Sunday brunch?

Yes — pool access at Barceló Al Jaddaf is included with your Ibiza Brunch booking at B-Heaven.

Where is B-Heaven rooftop in Dubai?

B-Heaven is on the rooftop of Barceló Al Jaddaf, Oud Metha Road, Al Jaddaf, Dubai — a 5-minute walk from Al Jaddaf Metro Station, with valet parking available.
