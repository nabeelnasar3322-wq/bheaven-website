Jazz Friday at B-Heaven Rooftop Dubai

Every Friday from 9 PM, B-Heaven transforms into Dubai's favourite rooftop jazz venue. Vocalist Anetta delivers intimate live jazz and soulful performances on the terrace of Barceló Al Jaddaf, with panoramic Dubai skyline views as your backdrop.

Arrive as the sun sets, sip on handcrafted cocktails and let the music carry the night. Tables fill quickly — book ahead on WhatsApp to secure your spot for the city's most atmospheric live jazz night.

B-Heaven is a Levantine rooftop restaurant and bar on the 10th floor of Barceló Al Jaddaf, Oud Metha Road, Al Jaddaf, Dubai.

Jazz Friday Dubai — FAQs

When does Jazz Friday happen?

Jazz Friday takes place every Friday night, starting at 9 PM and running until midnight, at B-Heaven rooftop, Al Jaddaf, Dubai.

Who performs at Jazz Friday Dubai?

Anetta is the resident Jazz Friday vocalist at B-Heaven, performing live jazz and soulful music every Friday night on the rooftop terrace.

How much does Jazz Friday cost?

Entry is AED 99 for ladies and AED 149 for gents, redeemable against food and drinks at the venue.

How do I book a table for Jazz Friday?

Book instantly via WhatsApp at +971 50 612 9150. Tables are limited — advance booking is strongly recommended.

Where is B-Heaven rooftop in Dubai?

B-Heaven is located on the rooftop of Barceló Al Jaddaf, Oud Metha Road, Al Jaddaf, Dubai. It is accessible from Al Jaddaf Metro Station (5 minutes walk) and by taxi or valet.

Is Jazz Friday a live jazz or DJ night?

Jazz Friday features a live vocal performance by Anetta — not a DJ. It is an intimate rooftop jazz experience with live music against the Dubai skyline backdrop.
