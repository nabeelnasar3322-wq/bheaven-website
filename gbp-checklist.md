### 18. Review management
- [ ] Respond to every review — positive and negative — within 48 hours
- [ ] For negative reviews: acknowledge, apologise briefly, offer to resolve offline. Never argue.
- [ ] Ask happy guests to leave a review (verbally, or via follow-up WhatsApp/email)
- [ ] Do NOT incentivise reviews (against Google policy)

**Response template for positive reviews:**
> Thank you so much, [Name]! We're glad you enjoyed [specific thing they mentioned]. We hope to see you again soon at B-Heaven — come for Jazz Friday or Ibiza Brunch next time! 🙌

**Response template for negative reviews:**
> Hi [Name], thank you for taking the time to share your feedback. We're sorry your experience didn't meet expectations — that's not the standard we hold ourselves to. Please reach out to us directly at [email] so we can make it right.
