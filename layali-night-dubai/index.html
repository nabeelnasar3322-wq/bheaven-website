Layali Night at B-Heaven Rooftop Dubai

Every Saturday from 9 PM, B-Heaven turns into a rooftop evening of classic Arabic songs. Nouha Issa performs live on the terrace of Barceló Al Jaddaf, with shisha, late-night plates and the Dubai skyline around you.

Entry is free and you dine à la carte. A reservation is required, so book ahead on WhatsApp to secure your table for the night.

B-Heaven is a Levantine rooftop restaurant and bar on the 10th floor of Barceló Al Jaddaf, Oud Metha Road, Al Jaddaf, Dubai.

Layali Night Dubai: FAQs

When does Layali Night happen?

Layali Night takes place every Saturday night, from 9 PM to 1 AM, at B-Heaven rooftop, Al Jaddaf, Dubai.

Who performs at Layali Night?

Nouha Issa performs classic Arabic songs live at Layali Night.

How much does Layali Night cost?

Entry is free. Guests dine à la carte and can order shisha. A reservation is required.

How do I book a table for Layali Night?

Book via WhatsApp at +971 50 612 9150. Booking ahead is required.

Where is B-Heaven rooftop in Dubai?

B-Heaven is located on the rooftop of Barceló Al Jaddaf, Oud Metha Road, Al Jaddaf, Dubai. It is a 5-minute walk from Al Jaddaf Metro Station, and reachable by taxi or valet.
