Grape & Graze at B-Heaven Rooftop Dubai

Every Thursday from 8 PM, Grape & Graze pairs a cheese board and Levantine mezze selections with two glasses of wine, white, rosé or red, for AED 99 per person.

It is a relaxed way to start the weekend on the rooftop of Barceló Al Jaddaf. Tables fill up, so book ahead on WhatsApp.

B-Heaven is a Levantine rooftop restaurant and bar on the 10th floor of Barceló Al Jaddaf, Oud Metha Road, Al Jaddaf, Dubai.

Grape & Graze Dubai: FAQs

When is Grape & Graze?

Grape & Graze takes place every Thursday from 8 PM at B-Heaven rooftop, Al Jaddaf, Dubai.

What is included in Grape & Graze?

A cheese board, Levantine mezze selections and two glasses of wine (white, rosé or red).

How much does Grape & Graze cost?

It is AED 99 per person.

How do I book a table for Grape & Graze?

Book via WhatsApp at +971 50 612 9150. Booking ahead is recommended.

Where is B-Heaven rooftop in Dubai?

B-Heaven is located on the rooftop of Barceló Al Jaddaf, Oud Metha Road, Al Jaddaf, Dubai. It is a 5-minute walk from Al Jaddaf Metro Station, and reachable by taxi or valet.
